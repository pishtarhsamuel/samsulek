## What this means for the bot

1. Network and sending costs are small if, and only if, we skip the 0.001 SOL tip services. Base fee plus a capped priority fee costs 0.03-0.27% per round trip at today's levels (M2). Every "premium" path (Helius Sender Max, tx.jup.ag, Nozomi, bloXroute protected) costs 3.33% per tx, 6.67% per round trip: that alone breaks the 5% rule. Only Jito (1,000 lamports) and Helius SWQOS-only (5,000 lamports) fit the budget.
2. The real budget killers are elsewhere: (a) token-account rent of ~5% of the position per new token (fact 43); it comes back only if we sell everything and close the account, and is lost with the position if the token is frozen or unsellable; (b) Jupiter's 50 bps fee on tokens less than 24 hours old (1% per round trip on /order); (c) venue fees and price impact (not measured here; see the venues research); (d) failed attempts, which still pay base + priority fees (0.02-0.12% each).
3. Priority-fee estimation as briefed ("recent estimates" on program IDs) returns zeros (M1). Use the trade's own writable accounts (bonding curve or pool, plus the fee recipient you will write-lock), one account per call, take about p75 of the last 150 blocks (~32 s), and clamp it. Never follow a max: the measured max would have cost 101-202% of the trade for a single tx.
4. Time is shorter than the docs say: ~214 ms slots mean a blockhash is valid ~32 s. Timeouts must be expressed in block height, not seconds. Alpenglow makes "finalized" arrive about as fast as "confirmed" (observed); still fetch blockhashes at confirmed.
5. Reading the chain must handle v1 transactions now (46% of txs): every getTransaction/getBlock in the reconciler needs maxSupportedTransactionVersion: 1, or it fails closed for the wrong reason. Building v1 is optional; v0 still works.
6. MEV, honestly: there is no public mempool, so a sandwich needs someone who sees our tx before it lands (a leader or relay with private order flow; multi-block "wide" sandwiches exist). A sandwich can take at most our slippage tolerance: with a 3% cap on a 0.03 SOL buy that is at most 0.0009 SOL (~$0.15) per trade. Tight minimum-out caps are the main protection; jitodontfront (free) only protects inside Jito bundles; Helius mev-protect (free query parameter) avoids flagged validators. Nothing guarantees zero. We must never front-run or sandwich anyone ourselves.
7. Landing: unstaked RPC sends compete for ~20% of leader capacity (fact 34). Free routes into staked capacity exist (Helius SWQOS-only, keyless 1 req/s; Jito 1 req/s per region). Real landing rates for our setup are unknown until measured with real sends in a later, owner-approved phase.

|---|---|---|
| Bonding curve (pump.fun, not graduated) | Direct pump.fun buy/sell instruction (official SDK or hand-built from the published IDL); Jupiter /build quote as a cross-check | /order adds 50 bps (token < 24 h); direct has the fewest moving parts |

- Send path: sign once, send the same bytes to our own RPC (maxRetries 0) and to Helius Sender SWQOS-only in Frankfurt (http://fra-sender.helius-rpc.com/fast?swqos_only=true&mev-protect=true) with a 5,000-lamport tip; rebroadcast every 1-2 s until confirmed or block height > lastValidBlockHeight. Add Jito Frankfurt only if later measurements show it lands better; a tip is paid whenever the tx lands, whichever path carried it, so put only one tip in a tx.
- Tip policy: no tip unless the path requires it; never a 0.001 SOL tip. Suggested caps (owner decides): CU price at most 200,000 uL/CU with CU limit = 1.2x simulation (observed 78k-208k CU on pump.fun, 97k-156k on PumpSwap), i.e. at most ~30,000 lamports (0.1%) priority per tx, and total landing spend at most 0.2% of the position per tx. If that does not land, skip the trade rather than escalate.
- Confirmation and retries: persist the signed tx and its signature before sending; resend the same tx only; re-quote and re-sign only after the old blockhash has provably expired and the signature is still unknown (otherwise double-buy risk); reconcile every trade from chain with getTransaction (maxSupportedTransactionVersion: 1).
- Expected all-in execution cost per round trip at today's fees (direct route, SWQOS-only tip, priority between the measured p75 and the cap, 150k CU): 2 x (5,000 base + 151 to 30,000 priority + 5,000 tip) = 20,300-80,000 lamports = 0.07%-0.27% of 0.03 SOL. Via Jupiter /order: plus 0.2% (mature) or 1.0% (new token) plus Jupiter's own landing fees (UNVERIFIED). Not included: venue fees, price impact, failed attempts, and the ~5% rent lockup per new token.

Contradictions with the owner's brief: (1) "MEV-protected submission where available within budget": most MEV-protected paths cost 0.001 SOL per tx (3.33%), only Jito and Helius SWQOS-only/mev-protect fit; (2) priority fees from program-ID filters return 0, so the estimator must use writable accounts; (3) the confirmation window is ~32 s and must be tracked in block height; (4) the 5% round-trip rule is met by execution costs but the ~5% token-account rent per new position equals the whole budget unless it is reliably recovered; (5) Solana's own docs on slot time (400 ms) and finality (~32 slots) are out of date.
